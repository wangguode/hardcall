Privacy Policy & B2B Data Processing Disclosure

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Controller: Wang Guo (HardCall Voice Architecture) · Website: https://hardcall.statementflow.win

1. Browser WebAudio Sandbox (Zero Microphone Audio Upload)

When you enable microphone input inside the HardCall Full-Duplex Barge-In Lab on hardcall.statementflow.win, your browser processes microphone PCM frames strictly in local memory via the Web Audio API (AudioContext + AnalyserNode) to compute real-time RMS acoustic energy and trigger local speech-onset cutoff timing (51.96ms Raw RTP mode vs. 920ms WebSocket mode).

Zero voice recordings or raw audio buffers are ever uploaded to HardCall servers from the browser sandbox. Benchmark timing runs saved in the sandbox are stored locally in your browser's localStorage (hardcall_bench_runs_v1) and can be cleared by you with one click at any time.

2. B2B Records & Edge Telemetry Stored in Cloudflare D1

To fulfill commercial engagements, deliver sample traces, and monitor service reliability, our Cloudflare Workers backend stores minimal structured records in Cloudflare D1:

  • Commercial Orders (hc_orders): Checkout and order identifiers (order_id, order_ref), customer work email, purchased tier (audit or kit), USD amount, payment status, optional telephony stack note, and an activation restore_token.
  • Pre-Sales Spot-Check & Invoice Leads (hc_leads): Work email, submitted test phone number / SIP URI / telephony stack summary, optional engineering notes, and coarse 2-letter ISO country header (cf-ipcountry) when you submit the lead-capture or invoice request form.
  • Paid Kickoff Intake (hc_intake_submissions): Verified order reference, work email, designated test SIP/WebRTC endpoint or GitHub username (for private repository provisioning), and pipeline latency notes submitted via /api/b2b-intake.
  • First-Party Funnel Telemetry (hc_funnel_events & pixel.js): Aggregate product interaction events (such as page_view, sim_start, checkout_start, and b2b_lead_capture) with coarse country code and truncated HTTP referrer, plus our first-party conversion measurement script (https://pulse.statementflow.win/api/pixel.js). We do not use third-party ad-network trackers, cross-site behavioral profiling cookies, or sell customer data to brokers.

3. Self-Hosted VPC Zero Audio Egress

Production deployments of the $1,200 HardCall 8kHz RTP Self-Hosted Deployment Kit execute 100% inside your own AWS, GCP, Azure, or bare-metal VPC alongside your FreeSWITCH / SIP infrastructure. The kit contains zero phone-home telemetry, zero external license callouts, and zero audio or SIP signaling egress to HardCall, supporting your internal HIPAA, SOC 2, and PCI-DSS isolation boundaries.

4. Sub-Processors (Creem.io, Cloudflare, Resend)

  • Creem.io (Merchant of Record): Processes credit cards, billing addresses, and corporate tax/VAT receipts directly under PCI-DSS compliance; HardCall never sees or stores payment card numbers.
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1: Hosts static assets, edge rate-limiting (ephemeral in-memory IP counters reset every 60 seconds), and encrypted-at-rest D1 tables described in Section 2.
  • Resend: Delivers transactional kickoff links, order confirmations, and requested RTP diagnostic traces to your work email.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Diagnostic call traces captured during a 48h SIP/WebRTC Latency Audit are used solely to produce your engineering report and are purged within 30 days of engagement completion (or immediately upon written request). You may request access, export, correction, or deletion of any lead, intake, or non-statutory order metadata associated with your email address by contacting hello@statementflow.win (fulfilled within 7 business days).
