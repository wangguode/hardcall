Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Operator: Wang Guo (HardCall) · Website: https://hardcall.statementflow.win

1. Information We Process

HardCall is an AI-powered voice interview drill simulator and coaching tool. When you run a voice drill session in your browser or native client, microphone audio and speech-to-text transcripts are processed ephemerally solely to evaluate pacing, filler words, barge-in interruption timing, and post-call coaching feedback.

We do not sell personal data, rent user recordings, or permanently store raw voice audio on our servers.

2. Payment & Billing Data (Creem.io)

Commercial B2B engagements (Voice Latency Audit Pilot, 8kHz RTP Deployment Kit, and Custom Enterprise Integrations) may be invoiced directly or processed via our Merchant of Record, Creem.io. Credit card numbers, billing addresses, and tax invoices are collected and secured directly by Creem.io under their privacy and PCI-DSS compliance standards. HardCall receives only order status, plan tier, and customer email metadata via signed webhooks to fulfill your engagement.

3. Local Storage & Telemetry

Session preferences, language selection, and local drill transcripts may be stored in your browser's localStorage for convenience. You may clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of any account or order metadata associated with your email address at any time by contacting us at hello@statementflow.win.
